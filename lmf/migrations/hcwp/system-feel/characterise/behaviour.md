## Age and current status — G03, G04

AgeResolver.GetAge uses DateTime.Now unless DateOfDeath supplies the threshold. Compute month difference by year/month (not completed birthday), years = months / 12. Under one year and months <=3, truncate total days: below 1 -> `0 day`, exactly 1 -> `1 day`, 2–6 -> `n days`, 7–13 -> `1 week`, otherwise whole truncated days /7 -> `n weeks`. Above three month-difference and under one year -> `n months`; otherwise `n Years m Months` using remainder months. It does not enforce future-birth validation or a birthday-adjusted age. Reference adaptation must inject one controlled clock at the Now site and exercise branch boundaries and death threshold. [src:C012]

The web model still computes AgeString for stillbirth; the summary view suppresses age to `-` when CurrentStatusCode equals literal `7`, and selects AgeAtDeath label when DateOfDeath.HasValue. Separately PersonTO retrieval nulls age for CurrentStatus_Stillbirth and invokes unavailable Oracle Lookups.Age otherwise. These are distinct source paths, not proof that the unavailable procedure equals AgeResolver; SF-G03 records this ambiguity. CurrentStatusResolver returns empty on null/empty else calls unavailable LookupsUtility.CurrentStatusText; raw status and status date are mapped separately. Status-dependent alert triggers are below.
